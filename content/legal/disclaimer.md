## AI-generated content may be wrong

Search results, details, adaptability assessments, rights-status indications, reports and illustrations are generated wholly or partly by artificial intelligence (currently Google Gemini and Imagen models). They may be inaccurate, incomplete, out of date or invented, including facts about authors, publishers, sales, existing adaptations, options, rights holders, budgets and comparable titles. Illustrations are AI-generated and are not official cover art. Always check important facts against reliable sources.

## No guarantee that any right is available

A rights-status indication is an estimate, never a guarantee.

- **Public domain** is assessed under United States copyright rules for published works (in 2026, works first published in 1930 or earlier). Copyright terms differ between countries, and a translation, edition, illustration, sequel, character or trademark connected with a work may still be protected.
- **Likely available** means only that Screendibs did not identify an option or other claim. It does not mean that any right is free, unencumbered or obtainable.
- **Optioned or held** and **Unverified** are also estimates based on the information available to the AI.

Before you make an offer, sign an agreement, pay for an option or invest in a project, confirm the rights position and chain of title with an entertainment rights representative or attorney.
