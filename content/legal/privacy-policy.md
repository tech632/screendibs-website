## 3. Personal information we collect

### Information you give us

- **Account information:** your email address and password when you create an account. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see it in readable form.
- **Personalisation information (optional):** if you complete onboarding or use personalisation features, the genres you like or avoid, films and books you like, the formats you work in (for example film, television or stage), your budget range, a description of your mandate or of what you are looking for, and your "swipes" (whether you liked or passed on suggested titles, with the title, author, year, type, genre and time).
- **Searches and requests:** what you type into the search assistant and other requests you make, and the works you view, unlock or add to your watchlist.
- **Purchases:** the plan you choose, the reports and unlock packs you buy, and the titles you unlock. Your card details are entered directly into Stripe's secure payment form. Stripe also collects billing details such as your name, country and postal code where needed.
- **Report delivery:** the email address to which you ask us to send a report.
- **Messages:** what you write to us by email or through the Website contact form, such as your name, email address, organisation and message.

### Information we collect automatically

- **Technical information:** IP address, browser type, device and operating system, the pages and features you use, timestamps, and error and server logs, collected by our hosting and authentication providers.
- **Sign-in information:** a unique user identifier and sign-in tokens created by Firebase Authentication and stored in your browser so that you stay signed in.
- **Usage counters:** the number of searches and free unlocks you have used, so that we can apply plan limits.
- **Fraud-prevention signals:** when you use a payment page, Stripe collects device and browsing information to detect and prevent fraud.

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics or social media tracking pixels. See our Cookie Notice.

### Information we receive from others

- **Stripe** tells us whether a payment succeeded, the status of your subscription and limited card information (such as card brand, last four digits and expiry date).
- **Business contacts.** For our business-to-business outreach, we may collect the professional contact details of people working in the entertainment industry (name, job title, company, business email address and publicly available professional information) from public sources such as company websites and professional directories. ## 4. How we use personal information and our legal bases

We use personal information only for the purposes below, or for purposes compatible with them, or as the law requires. For people in the European Union or the United Kingdom, we give the GDPR legal basis for each purpose.

- **To create and manage your account and sign you in.** Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.
- **To provide the Platform:** run searches, show details, adaptability assessments and rights-status indications, generate reports, deliver them as PDF files and by email, and send the rights-status notifications you ask for. Legal basis: contract.
- **To personalise results** using your onboarding answers, swipes and recent searches. This is optional and only happens if you choose to provide this information. Legal basis: consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
- **To take payments, manage subscriptions, unlocks, credits and plan quotas, and keep accounting records.** Legal basis: contract and legal obligation (tax and accounting law).
- **To communicate with you** about your account, purchases, changes to our terms and support requests. Legal basis: contract and legitimate interests.
- **To send marketing emails** about Screendibs. Legal basis: consent, or where the law allows, our legitimate interests (for business contacts). Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
- **To keep the Platform secure,** prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our terms and limits, and fix errors. Legal basis: legitimate interests and legal obligation.
- **To improve the Platform,** using aggregated or de-identified information where possible. Legal basis: legitimate interests.
- **To contact business prospects** about our services. Legal basis: legitimate interests, and consent where required by anti-spam law.
- **To comply with the law** and respond to lawful requests from authorities, and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Legal basis: legal obligation and legitimate interests.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have balanced our interests against your rights. You can ask us for more information about this assessment.

Under Quebec and Canadian law, we collect only the information necessary for these purposes and we rely on your consent, which may be implied where the purpose is obvious and the information is not sensitive. If we want to use your information for a new purpose that is not compatible with these, we will ask for your consent first, unless the law allows otherwise.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the following rights. Quebec law, PIPEDA and the GDPR give rights that overlap but are not identical. We apply them in a consistent way for everyone where we reasonably can.

- **Access:** to know whether we hold personal information about you and to get a copy.
- **Correction:** to have inaccurate, incomplete or ambiguous information corrected.
- **Deletion:** to have your information deleted where it is no longer needed, was collected unlawfully, or you withdraw consent, subject to legal retention obligations.
- **Withdrawal of consent:** to withdraw consent at any time, for example to personalisation or marketing emails. This does not affect processing before withdrawal, and may mean we can no longer offer some features.
- **Portability:** to receive information you gave us in a structured, commonly used technological format, or to have it sent to another organisation.
- **De-indexation (Quebec):** to ask us to stop disseminating your information or to de-index a hyperlink to it, where the law allows.
- **Information about automated processing:** to be informed of, and to comment on, any decision based exclusively on automated processing of your information. We do not currently make such decisions.
- **Objection and restriction (EU and UK):** to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and always to direct marketing, and to ask us to restrict processing in certain cases.
- **Complaint:** to complain to us and to a supervisory authority (see section 15).
