# In-app beta notice (draft text, as shipped in `app/src/components/BetaNotice.jsx`)

**Screendibs is in beta.**
We're still testing. You won't be charged during the beta, and search stays free.

*How we use your data* (expands):

- Any checkout you see runs in test mode. No real payment is taken.
- We store your account details (email, name, date of birth) and your taste profile to run your account and personalize results and reports.
- Your searches and taste-profile answers are sent to our AI provider to generate results. We don't sell your data.
- Features, results and prices may change while we test. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

Checkout page adds: "Your card is only used to pay for reports. Search is always free." and "Beta: checkout runs in test mode. You won't be charged."

To confirm before launch: the "we don't sell your data" and "delete at any time" promises match the Privacy Policy, and a link to the Privacy Policy is added once it is published (the app has no legal page on `main`).
